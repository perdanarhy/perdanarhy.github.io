He received the B.S. degree in telecommunications engineering from the Electronic Engineering Polytechnic Institute of Surabaya, Surabaya, Indonesia, in 2012, the M.S. degree in electrical engineering from Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS), Surabaya, Indonesia, in 2014, and the Ph.D. degree from the Department of Software and Communication Engineering, Hongik University, Sejong, Republic of Korea, in 2025.

From 2015 to 2021, he was a Lecturer with Politeknik Negeri Malang, Malang, Indonesia. From 2025 to 2026, he was a Postdoctoral Researcher with the School of Information and Communication Engineering, Chungbuk National University, Republic of Korea. He is currently an Assistant Professor with the Department of Intelligent Computing, Dong-Eui University, Busan, Republic of Korea.

His research interests include signal processing, optimization and machine learning for wireless communications, routing protocols, and physical- and network-layer security for beyond-5G and 6G networks. He received the Best Researcher of the Year award from BK21, Hongik University, from 2023 to 2025. He also received the Best Paper Award at the 2024 IEEE International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Information and Communication (ICAIIC 2024).
